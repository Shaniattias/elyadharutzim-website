A growing market

We are now expanding into the Israeli market as well: wholesalers, distributors and grocery stores, supplied from our warehouse at Ashdod Port.

  • CustomersWholesalers, distributors and grocery stores

  • DealsPre-arrival, at a preferred price
How it works

From supplier to customer.

  1. Sourcing at origin

    Goods from international brands, from suppliers in Europe, Dubai, Turkey and Jordan.

  2. Shipping to Ashdod

    Containers arrive by sea at Ashdod Port.

  3. Clearance and regulation

    Port release, import, labelling and compliance with standards.

  4. Delivery to the customer

    From our warehouse to wholesalers and distributors, from a full carton to a whole container.
