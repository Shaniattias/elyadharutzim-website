Mining, metals and resources

The metal is
already here.

Green processing of tailings, slag and processing residues, mine development and physical metals trading, in eighteen countries on four continents.

13Bttailings per year
8,500storage facilities
217Bm³accumulated volume
0new excavation needed

Billions of tonnes of material already mined and left behind. The range in the literature: 7–14 billion tonnes a year. Sources: Global Tailings Review; Nature Reviews Earth & Environment (2024).

01

Mining and mine development

An active mining asset in Nyarugusu, Tanzania: a CIP plant processing 500 tonnes per hour, recovering gold from ore grading about 4.5 parts per million.

02

Green metal recovery

Two modules: speeding up leaching from months to weeks using air alone, and selective separation of over 99% for copper, gold, silver and platinum group metals. No cyanide and no organic solvents. Developed by Timna Elements, validated in the lab (TRL 3–4).

03

Physical trading

Direct purchasing from producers in Uganda, Zambia and Tanzania, with controlled logistics through to the refinery. Over $300M since 2022.
