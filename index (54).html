Mining and mine development

An active mining asset in Nyarugusu, Tanzania. The ore is crushed, ground in a ball mill and separated in a CIP plant.

02 / Metal recovery

Metal recovery, without new excavation

A green technology that separates and recovers metals from tailings and mining materials. It works at ambient temperature and pressure, with no cyanide and no organic solvents, turning mining residues into a resource of economic value.

  • FeedstockTailings, slag and processing residues
  • CyanideNone
  • New excavationNot required
03 / Trading

Physical metals trading

Direct purchasing from producers in Uganda, Zambia and Tanzania. Every bar is weighed and documented, then moves through controlled logistics to the refinery.

The process

From the ground to the bar.

  1. Visit and inspection

    We visit the site, check the infrastructure and meet the local teams.

  2. Sampling

    Samples of soil, ore and mined material are collected and recorded.

  3. Processing and recovery

    Crushing, grinding and separation, or green recovery from already-mined material.

  4. Bar and trade

    Weighing, documentation and controlled logistics to the refinery.
